WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Reliability is a chain of evidence, not a happy-path screenshot.

The public model focuses on four mechanisms that are easy to inspect and hard to fake with marketing language.

01

One trace

Request, authorization, payment, work, outcome and settlement stay attached to one synthetic identity.

02

Read before retry

An ambiguous transport response triggers state reconciliation instead of blind duplicate action.

03

Fail closed

Unknown external state and incomplete outcome evidence stop progress instead of being guessed away.

04

Settle on evidence

A finished work step is not treated as an accepted outcome. Settlement waits for verification.

PUBLIC ABSTRACTION

Request → authority → payment → work → outcome → settlement
